ShortsAI

A self-hosted, local-first AI clipping and YouTube publishing tool. Long recordings become short vertical clips. Nothing is published without explicit user approval or a user-configured automation rule.

How it works

ShortsAI analyzes audio and video, finds interesting moments, generates an editing plan, renders vertical clips with captions and selective effects, runs quality control, and prepares approved clips for publication through the official YouTube Data API v3.
