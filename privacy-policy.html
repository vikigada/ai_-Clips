1. What ShortsAI is

ShortsAI is a self-hosted, local-first video clipping and publishing application. It analyzes long-form video, creates short vertical clips, and, only after explicit user approval or a user-configured automation rule, uploads those clips to a YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API v3.

2. Information we process

  • Google account and channel information obtained through OAuth 2.0, such as the channel ID, channel title and channel thumbnail.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens used to upload approved clips and read channel statistics. Tokens are encrypted at rest.
  • YouTube video identifiers and statistics for videos uploaded through ShortsAI, such as views, likes and comments returned by the YouTube Data API.
  • Video and audio files that the operator uploads or records, together with derived clips, subtitles, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags.
  • Technical logs needed to operate the service, such as inference timings, errors, worker status and system metrics. Logs do not contain OAuth tokens.
  • Session cookies used to keep the operator signed in to the local dashboard.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate the operator and protect the dashboard.
  • To upload clips only when the operator approves them or a standing automation rule applies.
  • To read the connected channel name, thumbnail and video statistics.
  • To render clips, generate captions, run quality control and display results.
  • To diagnose errors and keep the local system reliable.

4. Legal basis and consent

The operator connects a Google account by granting OAuth consent. Uploads occur only after an explicit approval action or after the operator deliberately enables an automation rule. The operator can revoke access at any time in the ShortsAI settings or in the Google Account permissions page.

5. YouTube API Services

ShortsAI uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube integration, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and acknowledge the Google Privacy Policy. ShortsAI does not use browser automation, cookies or unofficial methods to access YouTube.

6. Storage, retention and security

ShortsAI is designed to run on the operator's own server. Video files and rendered clips are stored on that server. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest with a server-side key. Access tokens are never returned to the browser. The operator controls retention and may delete clips, disconnect channels or remove the stored data.

When a channel is disconnected, ShortsAI attempts to revoke the token with Google and deletes or disables the stored credentials. YouTube videos that were already uploaded remain in the operator's YouTube account and must be managed in YouTube Studio.

7. Sharing

ShortsAI does not sell personal data. Data is shared with Google/YouTube only as necessary to perform the requested upload and statistics operations. The self-hosted operator is responsible for any additional sharing configured outside ShortsAI.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect a YouTube channel in Settings → YouTube integration.
  • Revoke access at Google Account permissions.
  • Delete individual clips from the Shorts page.
  • Ask the operator to delete stored files, database records or logs.

9. Children

ShortsAI is not directed to children and is not intended for use by children under the age required by YouTube's Terms of Service.
